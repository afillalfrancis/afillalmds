From first lesson to licence

Four stages, and you move on when you’re ready.

  1. Trial lesson

    Meet your instructor, sit in the car and try the controls in a safe space.

  2. Learn the basics

    Steering, clutch and braking, then moving off and stopping on quiet streets.

  3. Real traffic

    Junctions, lane changes, parking and busier roads, step by step.

  4. Test day

    A mock test, then we take you to the test centre and wait with you.
